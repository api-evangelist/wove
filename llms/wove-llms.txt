# Wove

> Wove is an AI-powered trade compliance and automation platform for freight forwarders, customs brokers, importers, and manufacturers. Wove automates HTS classification, tariff calculations, trade document processing, and cargo risk scoring — helping global trade teams move faster, reduce errors, and stay compliant.

## What Wove Does

Wove provides AI infrastructure for global trade operations. Core capabilities include:

- **HTS Classification**: AI-powered classification of products to the correct Harmonized Tariff Schedule codes, with confidence scoring and audit trails

- **Tariff Calculation**: Real-time duty and tariff calculations including Section 301, Section 232, IEEPA tariffs, antidumping and countervailing duties, and preferential trade agreement rates

- **Trade Document Processing**: Automated extraction and validation of commercial invoices, packing lists, bills of lading, certificates of origin, and other trade documents

- **Cargo Risk Scoring**: AI-driven risk assessment for cargo insurance and compliance screening

- **IEEPA Tariff Refund Calculator**: Free tool for importers to calculate potential tariff refund exposure following the Supreme Court's 2026 IEEPA ruling

- **API-First Architecture**: All capabilities available via REST API for integration into customs management systems, ERP platforms, and logistics software

## Who Wove Serves

- **Freight Forwarders**: Automate document processing and classification workflows at scale

- **Customs Brokers**: Reduce manual classification effort and improve accuracy on complex entries

- **Importers**: Calculate tariff exposure, identify refund opportunities, and manage compliance across product catalogs

- **Manufacturers**: Assess landed cost, manage sourcing decisions under shifting tariff regimes, and classify new products at scale

- **Trade Compliance Teams**: Enterprise tools for Fortune 500 companies managing complex global supply chains
